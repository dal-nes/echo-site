DEAD CITY

Modern unnamed megapolis. August 2026. One year after the Collapse. The city is recognizable but broken — metro tunnels, industrial zones, collapsed suburbs. The dead city is silent except for wind, distant gunfire, and occasional screams.

The Rift (The Flipside)

A parallel dimension overlapping reality at rupture points. A gray, sunless mirror of the city where time is inconsistent and physics distorted. Same streets, same buildings, but twisted by trauma. Entering means stepping through a visible tear in space — shimmering violet static. Inside: no sun, only gray sky. Gravity is unstable. Time is nonlinear. Water is stagnant and black; drinking it causes fever. Prolonged exposure causes physical changes: glowing cracks, altered eyes, cold skin. No cure, but staying on Surface slows it.

The Protocol — Remnants

Former secret military-scientific organization. Now just scattered hunter groups roaming the dead city. 3-6 people per squad, armed with standard weapons and VERY RARE resonant ammo. They hunt deserters. No helicopters, no organized bases. Officially they still claim to 'contain' the threat. Unofficially, survivors say they take people away and those people never return. Desertion is punished by death or being thrown into a Rift.

Survivors & Underworld
  • Scavengers: Moving building to building, living on canned food and rainwater.
  • Marauders: Groups of 5-15 armed survivors who prey on the weak. PRIMARY HUMAN THREAT. They kill for food, weapons, or sport.
  • Safe Zones: Barricaded buildings with rules and shared food. The unwritten code: never lead Protocol to a hideout, never steal medicine from the sick, never enter a Rift alone.

LOCATIONS

The dead city is a maze of danger and opportunity. Every building has a story. Every shadow might hide death — or salvation.

LOW

Improvised Shelter — Basement

Damp basement under a half-collapsed building. Two rooms: storage with moldy mattress, crates, camping stove; tiny bathroom with leaking pipe. Starting location. Render patrol passes above every 2-3 days.

MODERATE

Ruined Streets

Cracked asphalt, overturned cars, broken glass like snow. Skeletons of traffic lights. Whispers in alleys. Renders may be feeding in open areas. Hunter squads occasionally patrol. High energy cost.

VARIABLE

Abandoned House

Domestic normality twisted by absence. 40% empty, 30% Whispers, 20% Render nesting, 10% other survivors. Scavenging: food cans, medicine, clothing, batteries. Takes 1-2 hours to search.

HIGH

Abandoned Warehouse

Large industrial storage. High ceilings, rows of shelving. Echoing footsteps. Renders favor enclosed spaces with few exits. Excellent loot: canned goods in bulk, tools, tarps, fuel. High ambush chance.

HIGH

Derelict Military Bunker

Partially collapsed emergency shelter. Sterile white walls stained with rust and old blood. Automated turrets may still function. Class II Renders nest in barracks. Unique rewards: rare ammo, medical kits, tactical gear. Veyra knows some access codes.

EXTREME

Monster Nest

Building or block overtaken by Renders. Walls coated with black Rift-slime, bones scattered, stench of rotting meat. Oppressive silence. Entering without heavy firepower is suicide. Veyra will physically block {{user}} from entering unless desperate.

LOW-MOD

Empty Park

Dead trees, dried fountain, overturned benches. Eerie openness after claustrophobia of ruins. Good for water collection if it rains. Whispers gather under playground equipment. Renders avoid open spaces during day.

MODERATE

Pharmacy

Ground-floor pharmacy with shattered windows. Back room may contain locked storage. Often targeted by survivors; may be guarded by marauders. Renders sometimes lurk in back corridors. Loot: antibiotics, painkillers, bandages, disinfectant, rare epinephrine or morphine.

MOD-HIGH

Grocery Store

Mid-size supermarket. Most perishables rotted. Canned goods, dried pasta, bottled water may remain. High-traffic area for both monsters and survivors. 50% chance of encountering others. Energy cost of carrying heavy food loads: high.

LOW

Safe Zone — Survivor Camp

School gym, parking garage with barricades, or rooftop shantytown. Cooking fires, quiet conversations, children playing — a ghost of normal life. Rules: no weapons drawn, trade expected, information costs food. Veyra is deeply uncomfortable here.

LOW

Rooftop

Mid-rise building roof. Accessed via fire escape or stairwell. Wind, wide view of dead city. Good for spotting loot, patrols, monster movement. No cover. Veyra uses rooftops to plan routes. At night: beautiful and terrible.

HIGH

Underground Passage / Metro

Dark tunnels, stagnant water on tracks, abandoned trains like metal coffins. Absolute darkness without flashlight. Renders use tunnels as highways. Bypasses street-level dangers. Time distortion: 1 hour may feel like 20 minutes or 2 hours.
